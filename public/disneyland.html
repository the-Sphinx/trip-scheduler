🗺️ Two game-plans — which land to start with (tap)

Pink = lands · blue = rides.

① Recommended — start NORTH: Fantasyland + Toontown

Everyone rope-drops right into Tomorrowland (Baymax/Monsters) and sprints west to Splash, so Fantasyland's headliner Pooh's Hunny Hunt and the dark rides build the day's worst lines by mid-morning. Clear the north first while the crowd is east.

  • At tap-in (one person books for all 4): free Priority Pass → Pooh's Hunny Hunt (longest free line) + DPA → Splash Mountain (¥1,500, redeem an early-afternoon window).
  • Toontown at opening — Gadget's Go Coaster → Roger Rabbit → Meet Mickey (dead-end corner, fills late morning, so hit it first).
  • Fantasyland — Peter Pan's Flight first (its standby explodes fastest), then Snow White → Pinocchio; redeem the Pooh free pass at its window.
  • When the free-pass 120-min cooldown clears, grab Big Thunder Mountain for later.
  • Westernland — Big Thunder (free pass) + Tom Sawyer Island → Critter Country — Splash on your DPA.
  • Adventureland on the way back to the exit — Pirates → Western River Railroad → Jungle Cruise.
  • Tomorrowland last — late-afternoon Monsters/Buzz/Star Tours lines ease; grab a free pass if cooldown allows. (Skipping Baymax & Stitch.)

Fallbacks: Pooh free windows gone at tap-in → ride Pooh standby right at opening (before Toontown). · Peter Pan standby already >40 min → skip, catch it end-of-day. · Running behind → drop Tom Sawyer + Western River RR first, protect Big Thunder + Splash + Pooh. · DPA Splash sold out → ride it standby first thing (go Critter → Westernland first, flip the loop clockwise).

② The popular default — start EAST: Tomorrowland

What most guests do: rope-drop straight to Tomorrowland for the big names, then loop counter-clockwise back to the exit.

  • At tap-in: free Priority Pass → Monsters Inc. + DPA → Splash Mountain.
  • Tomorrowland at opening — Buzz → Monsters Inc (redeem free pass) → Star Tours. (Skip Baymax/Stitch.)
  • Fantasyland — grab the Pooh free pass once the 120-min cooldown clears; Peter Pan / Haunted Mansion / Snow White / Pinocchio standby.
  • Toontown — Gadget → Roger Rabbit → Meet Mickey.
  • Westernland — Big Thunder (free pass) + Tom Sawyer → Critter — Splash on DPA.
  • Adventureland on exit — Pirates → Western River RR → Jungle Cruise.

Trade-off vs ①: you knock out the east headliners first, but you rope-drop into the thickest crowd, and Pooh + Peter Pan standby balloon while you're in Tomorrowland — so you lean harder on the Pooh free pass and may not fit Peter Pan.
